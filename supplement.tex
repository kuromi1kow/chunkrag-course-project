\noindent This supplement gives implementation details and complete results for the analyses in the main paper. F1 and exact match are reported in points ($\times100$, two decimals); NDCG and proportions on the 0--1 scale (three decimals). The released CSV files keep full precision. Method labels follow the main paper: H (hybrid retrieval), U (unadapted reranker), B (document-level supervision) and L (literal-match supervision, condition E in the released files).
